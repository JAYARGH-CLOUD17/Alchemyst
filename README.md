PROJECT TITLE: Sun Son Solar Web System

TEAM NAME: Alchemyst

PROJECT DESCRIPTION:
Sun Son Solar Web System is a web-based system developed for Sun Son Solar to provide customers with an easier way to view and learn about their solar products and services. The system will organize important product information such as solar panels, inverters, batteries, and other solar-related products in one accessible platform.

The system will also help Sun Son Solar manage and present their products more efficiently while allowing customers to easily explore available solar solutions and submit inquiries. Overall, the system aims to improve the way Sun Son Solar presents its products and communicates with its customers.
